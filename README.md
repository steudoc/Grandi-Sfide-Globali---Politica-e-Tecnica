# Anatomy of a Short-Term Rental Platform: The Case of Airbnb

![Anatomy of Airbnb Banner](./cover.png)

## Description
A multidisciplinary case study developed for the **Politica e Tecnica (Grandi Sfide Globali)** course during my Bachelor's Degree in Computer Engineering at **Politecnico di Torino**. The report investigates the operational and socio-technical architecture of Airbnb, combining an analysis of its distributed cloud systems and machine learning pipelines with an evaluation of fiscal regulations, surveillance capitalism, data extraction mechanisms, and algorithmic digital discrimination.

---

## Core Competencies & Key Findings

* **Software Architecture & Cloud Infrastructure:** Analysis of Airbnb's microservices ecosystem orchestrated via API Gateways, real-time data pipelines (GraphQL, Nginx, Firebase), and AWS cloud hosting (S3, EC2, RDS, Presto, Druid, Airpal).
* **Machine Learning & Algorithmic Pricing:** Evaluation of dynamic pricing algorithms (*Smart Pricing*), personalized search ranking models, and automated fraud-detection mechanisms.
* **Fiscal Frameworks & Platform Regulation:** Exploration of the legal and fiscal landscape governing short-term rentals (e.g., Italian D.Lgs. 50/2017, flat tax regimes, and regulatory compliance vs. tax evasion disputes).
* **Data Capitalization & Mutual Surveillance:** Investigation into profile datafication, user telemetry, third-party data exploitation, and bidirectional rating systems acting as social credit/surveillance mechanisms.
* **Algorithmic Bias & Digital Discrimination:** Critical literature review and empirical assessment of algorithmic and human bias on the platform, evaluating racial/gender disparities in booking acceptance rates and listing prices.
